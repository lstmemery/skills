---

Load and follow skill `grilling` for the interview and skill `domain-modeling`
for durable language and decision records. As terms and decisions resolve,
update the glossary and qualifying ADRs under domain-modeling's rules.

Finish when the interview's shared-understanding check is complete and the
records reflect the accepted decisions, with unresolved proposals labeled.
When the requested outcome is a revised artifact, prepare that reviewable
artifact alongside the records; the documentation is not a substitute for it.
