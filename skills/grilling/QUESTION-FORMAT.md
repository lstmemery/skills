# Inline interview rounds

Use only when the harness has no interactive question facility. Present the
whole current frontier together, one numbered block per decision:

```markdown
**Q1 — <title>**
<Decision and two to five real alternatives, with their tradeoffs.>

Recommended: <choice and concise reason>.

---

**Q2 — <title>**
<Next independent decision and alternatives.>

Recommended: <choice and concise reason>.
```

Keep evidence outside option labels. Use multiple selection only where several
answers can apply together. A bare A/a accepts all recommendations for this
round under the rule in SKILL.md; otherwise record each answer separately.
An unanswered decision remains on the frontier.
